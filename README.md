## 🌏 About the Project

Planning a trip often means switching between multiple websites to check the best time to visit, find trustworthy accommodation, compare transport routes, locate food options, and remember what to pack. Important details can be scattered, difficult to verify, or missing altogether.

**Yatra-Saathi brings these planning tasks together in one travel-focused experience.** It is designed with Indian travellers in mind—from families and budget-conscious explorers to pilgrims and people visiting an unfamiliar destination for the first time.

> **Our idea:** Travel planning should be more than booking a ticket or room. Travellers should know what to expect, how to get there, and how to prepare before they leave.

## ✨ What Makes Yatra-Saathi Different?

| Typical travel-planning challenge | Yatra-Saathi's approach |
|---|---|
| Destination information is spread across different sources | Destination insights and travel preparation in one workspace |
| A hotel listing may not explain proximity to the place you want to visit | Landmark-oriented accommodation discovery and distance comparison |
| Route planning can involve several transport changes | Step-by-step, multi-modal journey guidance |
| Travellers may forget documents or essential items | A checklist tailored to destination, dates, transport, and travellers |
| Search results may not tell the whole story | Clear distinction between sourced facts, estimates, and AI suggestions |

## 🚀 Core Features

### 🗺️ 1. Smart Destination

Make a more informed decision before starting your trip.

- Select a state/Union Territory, destination, travel date, and traveller details.
- Explore recommended seasons and suitable times to visit.
- Review seasonal weather guidance, crowd outlook, and general cost tendencies where data is available.
- Get destination and activity suggestions based on the trip context.
- Understand how to reach a destination by train, bus, flight, or road.
- Find relevant railway stations, bus terminals, airports, and route information when supported by connected services.
- View pre-travel reminders and links to official guidance where available.

### 🏨 2. Hotels & Accommodation

Find accommodation with the destination—and the landmark that matters to you—in mind.

- Explore hotels, guest houses, homestays, lodges, and resorts.
- Search around places of interest, railway stations, bus stands, and airports.
- Filter by budget, accommodation type, rating, and listed facilities.
- View available listing photos, addresses, ratings, reviews, public contact details, and official websites.
- Compare distance and estimated travel time when reliable route data is available.

**Example:** A traveller visiting Amritsar can search around the Golden Temple rather than receiving the same generic hotel list for every destination.

### 🍽️ 3. Restaurants & Food Discovery

Explore food options around a destination or selected landmark.

- Filter by vegetarian/non-vegetarian options where reliably listed.
- Explore cuisines, price levels, ratings, and available facilities.
- Review addresses, public contact details, websites, and opening information when available.
- Compare proximity to selected landmarks or transport hubs.
- Use responsive cards designed to keep photos and restaurant details readable on desktop and mobile.

### 🚆 4. AI Trip Planner

Plan the journey—not just the destination.

- Enter a starting location and destination.
- Compare train, bus, flight, and road options when suitable data is available.
- Explore route alternatives such as budget-oriented, faster, or more convenient options.
- Understand multi-modal journeys, transfers, and last-mile travel where supported by verified information.
- Visualize the journey through a route map and step-by-step roadmap.
- Discover useful transport hubs and optional food stops along the route.
- Organize multi-day plans with an interactive timeline.
- Access relevant transport, hotel, and activity booking links where integrations are available.

### 🤖 5. YatraBot — Conversational Travel Assistant

Describe the trip you want in natural language.

Try requests such as:

- “Suggest a place with pleasant weather.”
- “I want somewhere less crowded.”
- “Find a peaceful nature destination.”
- “Suggest a family-friendly weekend trip.”
- “Recommend a destination near my location.”

YatraBot is designed to use the available destination information and trip context to provide more relevant suggestions. Location-based recommendations should use a location the user provides or explicitly permits the app to access.

### ✅ 6. AI Smart Travel Checklist

Prepare for a trip through a guided, step-by-step checklist.

The checklist can take into account:

- Destination and travel dates
- Trip duration
- Adults, children, and senior citizens
- Train, bus, flight, or personal vehicle
- Trip type and planned activities
- Special considerations entered by the traveller

Checklist categories include documents and IDs, packing, transport, destination-specific preparation, family needs, safety, and AI travel suggestions. Users can track completion, add custom items, and review their travel-readiness progress.

### 🌐 7. Designed for a Clear, Friendly Experience

- Bright, travel-oriented visual theme.
- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- Simple navigation between travel-planning workspaces.
- Clear loading, empty, and unavailable-data states.
- Subtle animations and visual route guidance.
- Consistent Yatra-Saathi branding.

### 🔎 About live data

Not every integration is necessarily active in every deployment. Actual features depend on valid API credentials, provider access, configuration, and usage limits.

- Google Maps Platform is a Google service.
- **SerpApi is a third-party provider, not an official Google API.**
- Google Places listings do not automatically provide live hotel-room availability or guaranteed booking prices.
- Live train, bus, and flight schedules, fares, and ticket availability require suitable transport data providers.
- Seasonal crowd guidance should not be described as live crowd measurement unless a reliable live source is connected.

Useful documentation:

- [Google Maps Platform](https://developers.google.com/maps)
- [Google Places API](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service)
- [Google Routes API](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes)
- [SerpApi Google Maps Local Results](https://serpapi.com/maps-local-results)
- [SerpApi Google Maps Place Results](https://serpapi.com/maps-place-results)

> **Note:** The commands above are a general setup guide. Follow the scripts and environment-variable names actually defined in the repository.

## 🔐 API Keys & Security

Never commit real API keys or secrets to GitHub.

Example environment-variable names (use only those supported by the current code):

```env
SERPAPI_KEY=your_serpapi_key
GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY=your_google_maps_key
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key
```

- Keep private keys in environment variables or a hosting platform's secret manager.
- Keep `.env` and `.env.local` out of version control.
- Commit placeholder values only in `.env.example`.
- Restrict API keys to the required APIs and permitted origins.
- Keep server-only secrets out of frontend JavaScript and public client bundles.
- If a secret is accidentally published, revoke or rotate it immediately.

## 🎯 Data Accuracy Principles

Yatra-Saathi is meant to support real travel decisions, so trustworthy information matters.

1. **No fabricated listings:** Do not invent hotel names, restaurant names, ratings, reviews, prices, photos, phone numbers, opening hours, or facilities.
2. **Clear sourcing:** Distinguish API-provided facts, user-provided details, estimates, and AI-generated recommendations.
3. **Honest live status:** Do not label information as live unless a connected source actually supplies it.
4. **Official requirements:** Verify permits, registrations, and mandatory documents through reliable official sources.
5. **Graceful fallback:** If a service is unavailable, explain that clearly instead of showing misleading results.
6. **Respect provider terms:** Follow API usage, caching, photo display, and attribution requirements.
